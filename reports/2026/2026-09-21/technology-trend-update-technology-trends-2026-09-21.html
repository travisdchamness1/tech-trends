Technology Trend Update — September 21, 2026

Observation window: 2026-09-18T07:59:17-07:00 to 2026-09-21T07:58:35-07:00

1. ABB launches source-to-rack DC power portfolio for AI data centers

ABB introduced Infinitus, a source-to-rack direct-current portfolio for AI data centers, including solid-state transformers, DC distribution and protection. ABB says first main components will be produced within 12 months and complete DC-native facilities are expected within two to three years.

Why it matters: Power delivery is becoming a first-order constraint as AI rack densities rise; an integrated commercial DC architecture from a major electrification supplier moves 800V-class designs toward deployable infrastructure.

Trend: AI infrastructure is forcing a redesign of the electrical stack from utility connection to rack.

Sources: ABB — Infinitus direct current portfolio for AI data centers

2. FDA updates regulations to permit innovative non-animal drug-testing methods where appropriate

FDA issued a direct final rule clarifying that non-animal methods can be used where appropriate in preclinical safety testing for drugs and biologics, including human-cell systems, organs-on-chips and computer models.

Why it matters: Regulatory acceptance is a gating condition for wider commercial use of advanced in-vitro and computational testing platforms in drug development.

Trend: Biopharma development infrastructure is shifting toward human-relevant experimental and computational models alongside traditional animal testing.

Sources: FDA — Regulations updated to advance alternatives to animal testing

3. Marvell demonstrates 2 nm optical interconnect technology for next-generation AI data centers

Marvell announced 2 nm demonstrations spanning 400G/lane PAM4, 800G ZR/ZR+ with MACsec and 1.6T ZR/coherent-lite technologies aimed at AI scale-up, scale-out and scale-across connectivity.

Why it matters: As accelerator clusters grow, optical bandwidth and power per bit increasingly constrain useful compute. Moving multiple optical DSP classes to 2 nm is a concrete hardware step toward 3.2T connectivity.

Trend: AI infrastructure bottlenecks are shifting from accelerator availability toward data movement, optics and power efficiency.

Sources: Marvell — Industry-first 2nm optical technology demos for AI data centers

4. Kodiak and PrePass integrate driverless trucks with roadside enforcement infrastructure

Kodiak and PrePass announced an automated process connecting enhanced autonomous-truck inspection information to state roadside screening and enforcement systems, with initial implementation beginning in Texas and Louisiana.

Why it matters: Scaling autonomous trucking across states requires regulatory and roadside operating infrastructure, not only autonomous-driving capability. This integration addresses that deployment seam directly.

Trend: Autonomous trucking is moving from vehicle autonomy toward the institutional infrastructure required for interstate commercial operation.

Sources: Kodiak AI / PrePass — Driverless trucking enforcement integration

5. Faraday Future puts nine embodied-AI robot configurations on sale for immediate delivery

Faraday Future launched nine embodied-AI robot configurations across five models and four packaged industry solutions for education, research, security and inspection, stating that all nine products are available for sale and delivery.

Why it matters: Commercial availability is stronger evidence than a prototype announcement and broadens the set of embodied-AI products being offered into practical vertical workflows.

Trend: Embodied AI is moving from demonstrations toward packaged products and vertical deployment solutions.

Sources: Faraday Future — Nine EAI devices and four industry solutions launched
