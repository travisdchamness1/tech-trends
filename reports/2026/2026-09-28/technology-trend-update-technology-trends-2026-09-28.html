1. SpaceX Starship reaches orbit for the first time and deploys Starlink V3 satellites

On Flight 14, Starship reached Earth orbit for the first time after 13 integrated flights that used suborbital trajectories. The mission also deployed the first batch of Starlink V3 satellites, while Super Heavy completed a controlled Gulf splashdown.

Why it matters: Reaching orbit converts Starship from a suborbital flight-test vehicle into an orbital launch system and demonstrates the mission profile needed for operational Starlink V3 deployment.

2. NVIDIA launches an open full-stack safety platform for autonomous AI agents

NVIDIA launched Open Agent Safety Platform, combining the OpenShell secure runtime with the Sentry reference system design. Sentry uses BlueField-4 DPUs as an out-of-band watchdog, while more than 100 organizations are participating across software, infrastructure, critical industry and robotics.

Why it matters: Agent safety is moving from application-level policy into independent infrastructure controls spanning software and silicon.

NVIDIA — Open Agent Safety Platform

3. Vertical Aerospace opens Valo assembly centre for certification aircraft and early production

Vertical Aerospace opened a dedicated 60,000-square-foot Aircraft Assembly Centre at Cotswold Airport. The facility will assemble certification-conforming Valo aircraft and a hybrid-electric demonstrator and is designed to support more than 25 aircraft annually during early production after certification.

Why it matters: The facility moves Valo from prototype development toward certification-oriented industrialization and creates physical capacity for compliance aircraft and initial customer deliveries.
