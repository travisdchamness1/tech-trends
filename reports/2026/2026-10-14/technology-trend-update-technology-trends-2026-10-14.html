Principal signal: AI-era industrial capacity is advancing through semiconductor fab financing, dedicated power, power-conversion acquisitions, and robotic construction contracts. Many announced facilities are not yet operating.

1. GlobalFoundries selected to operate up to $21 billion U.S.-Japan semiconductor fab expansion

GlobalFoundries will operate two planned 300 mm fabs in Malta, New York; the first $10 billion investment targets 2029 operation and approximately 900,000 wafers annually. The second facility depends on additional investment.

Primary source: https://gf.gcs-web.com/news-releases/news-release-details/globalfoundries-selected-operate-two-new-semiconductor-fabs-new

2. AEP plans $4 billion, 702 MW fuel-cell project under 20-year Google power agreement

AEP plans a 702 MW Bloom Energy fuel-cell project in Wyoming under a 20-year Google PPA; construction is targeted for 2026 and full operation for late 2028.

Primary source: https://www.aep.com/news/stories/view/12314/

3. Flex completes EPC Power acquisition to integrate grid-forming conversion for AI data centers

Flex completed its acquisition of EPC Power, adding grid-forming and power-conversion technology to its data-center infrastructure portfolio.

Primary source: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/flex-completes-acquisition-of-epc-power-expanding-power-conversion-capabilities-for-ai-data-centers-and-grid-applications-302907691.html

4. Promise Robotics signs multi-year production agreement supporting more than 1,250 D.R. Horton homes

Promise Robotics will provide factory-as-a-service robotic manufacturing for production supporting more than 1,250 homes; its Phoenix factory is expected to begin manufacturing in early 2027.

Primary source: https://news.promiserobotics.com/promise-robotics-expands-physical-ai-production-infrastructure-for-u-s-homebuilding-through-multi-year-commercial-agreement-with-d-r-horton/

5. IBM and Siemens partner on design and lithography methods for 3D nanostack chips

IBM and Siemens announced research on EDA, process simulation, and lithography optimization to move IBM's experimental 3D nanostack transistor architecture toward manufacturability.

Primary source: https://newsroom.ibm.com/2026-10-14-ibm-and-siemens-collaborate-on-research-to-advance-nanostack-architecture
