3. Merck reports 6.9-month median survival improvement in Phase 3 endometrial cancer trial

Merck presented detailed TroFuse-005 data for investigational sacituzumab tirumotecan after prior platinum chemotherapy and PD-1/PD-L1 therapy. Median overall survival was 19.0 versus 12.1 months (hazard ratio 0.68); median progression-free survival was 7.2 versus 3.9 months. Grade 3+ treatment-related adverse events occurred in 54.4% versus 41.4%. The FDA accepted the biologics application for priority review with a June 15, 2027 target date.

Significance: The randomized 776-patient trial supplies quantitative overall-survival evidence in a difficult-to-treat population, with a meaningful toxicity tradeoff. The drug is not yet approved for this indication.

Trend: Targeted antibody-drug conjugates are demonstrating survival benefits in additional late-stage oncology indications.

4. Unconventional AI agrees to acquire NeuReality to accelerate alternative inference-chip development

Unconventional AI and NeuReality announced a definitive, board-approved acquisition agreement on October 26. NeuReality brings inference-system, silicon and data-movement engineering expertise; the deal is expected to close in Q4 2026, subject to conditions. Unconventional's 1,000x efficiency ambition remains an unproven research target.

Significance: The acquisition would add an experienced silicon-productization team to a research-stage alternative compute architecture. It is a concrete industrial consolidation step, not evidence that the advertised efficiency target has been achieved.

Trend: AI compute challengers are combining novel architecture research with production-oriented semiconductor and systems engineering.
